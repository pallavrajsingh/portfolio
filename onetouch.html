    Who uses it

    Many of them are on their first smartphone.

    1. India is the world's second-biggest smartphone market, with about 455 million people online every day.
    2. Yet in 2015, only 18 out of every 100 internet users had a smartphone.
    3. That number was expected to double to 36 by 2023…
    4. …and reach 60 by 2026, helped along by Digital India.
    5. So many OneTouch users will be holding a smartphone for the first time. The app has to make sense to them from the first tap.
    Apps people use today

    BHIM, UMANG and MyGov each cover part of it.

    OneTouch has all of it in one app.

          Visual design

          Colours, type and icons

          Blue gradient headers, a light sheet that slides up over them, and white cards with soft shadows. Shortcuts and stats get their own colours; everything else stays quiet. Click a swatch to copy its hex code.

          Aa
          Heading · 28/34 · BoldPay a bill
          Amount · 36/40 · ExtraBold₹1,894
          Body · 16/24 · SystemSaved bills show up on their own.
          Hindi · 16/24मेरे लिए योजनाएँ

          Headings and numbers in Plus Jakarta Sans; body text in the phone’s own font, SF Pro on iOS and Roboto on Android, with Noto Sans Devanagari for Hindi. Icons sit on a 24px grid and always come with a label.
